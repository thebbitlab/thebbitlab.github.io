Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 July 2026

Orbit (“the app”) is a private journaling app made by thebbit.lab. This policy explains what happens to what you write. The short version: it stays on your phone.

Your notes never leave your device

Everything you write is stored only on your device, encrypted at rest with a key held in your device’s secure keychain. There are no accounts, no servers, and no sync. We cannot read, access, or recover your notes — not for support requests, not for anyone.

What we collect

We collect nothing. thebbit.lab runs no servers, no accounts, and no analytics, so nothing about you or what you write ever reaches us. The one place data is collected is advertising, and it is collected by Google rather than by us — see “Ads” below.

Ads

Orbit is free, and you can earn paper clips by watching optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. An ad is requested only when you choose to watch one. To serve and measure ads, Google collects device and advertising identifiers, coarse location inferred from your IP address, device and app information, and ad-interaction data, under Google’s own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We never send Google — or anyone — your notes, captions, or anything you write.

Tracking & your choices

Before any ad is requested, Orbit asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, or never answer, Orbit requests non-personalized ads only. Where local law requires it (for example the EEA and UK), Google’s consent form is shown first and your choices there govern what Google may use. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you never watch an ad, no ad request is ever made.

Sharing

If you choose to share when burning a note, the app composes an image containing your caption and the note’s text, and hands it to the system share sheet. Content leaves your device only when you choose a destination there. What you share, and where, is entirely under your control.

Purchases

Paper-clip purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchase records are handled under Apple’s privacy policy.

Notifications

All notifications are local: scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No push tokens are created and nothing is sent to a server.

Backups

Your data is included in your device backup (iCloud or computer), protected according to your own backup settings. The encryption key travels in your keychain so restored backups keep working on a new phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is rated 12+ for infrequent mature themes users may write themselves.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship inside the app with a new date above. Questions: thebbit.lab@gmail.com.
