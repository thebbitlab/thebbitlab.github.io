Orbit

Write a thought, seal it into a capsule, and send it into orbit for 6, 12, 18, or 24 months. When it comes back you read it once, revisit it once, and then it burns.

Support

Questions, bug reports, or anything else — email thebbit.lab@gmail.com. It’s a small operation, so replies may take a few days.

Before you write in

Lost notes cannot be recovered. Orbit has no accounts and no servers — everything lives encrypted on your own device. Burned notes are permanently deleted, and neither we nor Apple can restore them. This is how the app is meant to work, but it does mean we can’t help retrieve anything that’s gone.

Moving to a new phone? Restore from your device backup and your capsules come with it, encryption key included.

Purchases are handled by Apple. For refunds or billing questions, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
